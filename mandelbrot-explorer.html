MANDEL//SCOPE

a deep-zoom Mandelbrot explorer · GPU perturbation renderer · zoom to 10290

What you're looking at

The Mandelbrot set: for every point c in the complex plane, iterate z → z² + c. Points that never escape are the black interior; colors show how quickly the rest escape. The boundary is infinitely detailed — this explorer lets you dive to a magnification of 10²⁹⁰ (about 960 zoom doublings), computed with one arbitrary-precision reference orbit plus per-pixel GPU deltas (perturbation theory, the same idea behind Kalles Fraktaler).

Fractals

MANDEL is the classic z → z²+c. JULIA fixes c and iterates from the pixel instead — every point of the Mandelbrot set owns its own Julia set: navigate somewhere interesting in MANDEL, then click JULIA to use that exact spot as c (shown under the chips). SHIP (Burning Ship) takes absolute values before squaring — angular, flame-like architecture; the famous little ship hides near re −1.75. TRICORN conjugates z each step. CARPET, TRIANGLE (Sierpiński) and KOCH (snowflake) are exactly self-similar geometry rather than escape-time chaos. Depth limits differ by the math involved: Mandelbrot and the Sierpiński sets go deepest, Ship and Tricorn to 10²⁵, Julia to 2.5×10¹³ (it switches to the exact CPU engine when your view centers on an escape lake — slower, but glitch-free), Koch least (it repeats identically anyway).

Navigation

Scroll / pinch zooms toward the cursor · drag pans · double-click dives 3×. Keyboard: + − zoom, ←→↑↓ pan, R reset view, H this help. While moving, the image renders at an adaptive quality that targets smooth framerates; the full-quality frame appears the moment you stop.

Palette & color

Palette chips hold the core presets; the MORE PRESETS… dropdown adds fourteen further looks; CUSTOM reveals four color pickers building a cyclic gradient. Density sets how fast colors cycle with iteration depth (higher = finer bands), Shift rotates the whole palette, Animate slowly cycles it live (negative = reverse), and Interior colors the set itself. Palette changes recolor instantly — nothing is recomputed.

Quality & engine

Detail multiplies the iteration budget (auto-scaled with zoom, up to 100k). If deep regions show smooth featureless voids where filaments belong, raise it. AA 2X/3X/4X renders 4 / 9 / 16 samples per pixel on still frames — calms the pixel "grain" in dense areas, gives clean edges on the geometric fractals, and helps video compression enormously. 4X at high output resolutions is capped by GPU surface limits automatically. GPU / CPU ONLY: the GPU perturbation engine covers all depths; the CPU fallback is a plain float64 renderer limited to ~2.5×10¹³.

Auto dive

AUTO DIVE lets the engine explore for you: it probes the view, steers toward the most intricate boundary structure (with a bit of randomness), and descends 12 orders of magnitude live on screen. Any click or scroll stops it. Great for finding video targets.

Zoom video

RENDER VIDEO treats your current view as the destination and renders a smooth exponential dive from the overview down to it — every frame full quality, at a fixed iteration depth so nothing flickers in. Duration is a fixed length or paced by zoom speed (seconds per ×10). Resolution up to custom 8K-ish, quality guided by the live bits-per-pixel readout (aim ≥ 0.3 bpp for showcase renders).

Formats: MP4 · H.264 plays everywhere. MP4 · HEVC gives more detail per megabit (falls back to H.264 if unsupported). WEBM · VP9 is ideal for browsers/YouTube. PNG SEQ is the master-quality path: lossless frames into a folder you pick, plus your soundtrack as WAV and an ENCODE.txt with ready ffmpeg commands — browser encoders halve color resolution (4:2:0); offline encoding from PNGs does not.

Renders continue at full speed in background tabs. Files estimated over ~800 MB stream directly to disk (a save dialog appears first); every render ends on a panel with a manual SAVE FILE button. Coordinates in video burns a location/zoom readout into the frames.

Color animation & music sync

OFF / FWD / REV / DRIFT cycle the palette during the video — DRIFT wanders organically, never repeating. Load a song with ♪ LOAD AUDIO: cycling speed follows the track's energy, beats kick the color phase and pulse brightness, Reactivity scales it all, Focus aims it — at 0 the whole image reacts, at 1 only the dense structures dance while fast-escaping surroundings stay steady (0 restores uniform reaction), and the soundtrack is muxed into the output (AAC/Opus) or written as WAV with PNG SEQ.

Photo export

SAVE PNG renders a still at any resolution (presets or custom up to 8192px) with the same framing as your screen — anti-aliased, full quality, wallpaper-ready.

Share / state

COPY / PASTE moves the complete state — position at full arbitrary precision, zoom, palette, every setting — through the clipboard as a compact string; SAVE / LOAD FILE does the same as .json. States restore pixel-exactly on any screen size. Copy your finds before diving on!

The HUD

Bottom-left: re/im center coordinates (digits grow with depth), zoom factor, iter current iteration budget, the active engine (GPU·pert = standard, GPU·pert-fx = extended-exponent deep tier below ~10²⁸, CPU·Nw = fallback) and the last frame's render time.

Limits worth knowing

Depth caps at 10²⁹⁰ (float-exponent range of the delta arithmetic). The 100k iteration ceiling means extremely deep, iteration-hungry spots can render as interior — minibrot-centered dives go deepest. Chaotic boundary pixels with 1000+ iteration escape times carry ~1–2% inherent float32 noise (invisible, but honest).
